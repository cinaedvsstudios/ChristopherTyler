FOREVER BOUND

AI-ASSISTED CINEMATIC PRODUCTION / VISUAL DEVELOPMENT / STORYTELLING

Forever Bound is an ongoing cinematic fantasy project developed through AI-assisted visual production, character development, world-building, motion, editing and sound.

The project explores how generative tools can be used as part of a wider production process rather than simply to create individual images. Characters, costumes, environments, lighting and visual style are repeatedly developed and refined so that material created at different stages still feels as though it belongs to the same cinematic world.

A central piece of the project is a completed five-minute opening sequence with sound, created as a finished audiovisual scene rather than a collection of concept images. It combines visual development, scene continuity, pacing, atmosphere, editing and sound to establish the characters and world.

Forever Bound demonstrates my ability to take AI-assisted material from concept and prompt direction through iteration, consistency, motion and post-production into a completed watchable sequence.
