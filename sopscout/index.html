SOPSCOUT + ZALANDO

AI ADOPTION / CREATIVE DIRECTION / INTERNAL VIDEO + COMMUNICATION

SOPScout is an internal AI-enabled operational assistant for Zalando Operations. I designed the creative launch and adoption experience around the tool, with the goal of making AI feel practical, approachable and relevant to everyday operational work.

I developed the visual identity and mascot, launch presentation, internal communication and access materials, alongside a playable browser-based engagement experience. The content translated the tool's capabilities, use cases and guardrails into something non-technical users could understand and engage with.

Alongside SOPScout, I have created other internal visual and video content for Zalando, including an end-of-year montage designed to bring together team moments and highlights into a concise, engaging audiovisual recap.

Together, this work demonstrates my ability to combine AI enablement, creative communication, video production and structured rollout thinking inside a professional Zalando environment.
