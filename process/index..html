PROCESS + TOOLS

AI as a production workflow, not a one-off output.

The Production Chain

01

CONCEPT

Define the core narrative, visual style, and constraints before touching any AI tools. Establish what the output actually needs to achieve.

02

REFERENCES

Curate mood boards and visual benchmarks to guide prompt engineering, establishing clear style targets and color palettes.

03

PROMPT DEVELOPMENT

Translate creative direction into structured prompts covering subject, composition, styling, lighting, camera language and continuity requirements.

04

GENERATION

Execute initial batches, testing different models and parameters to find the highest baseline quality and stylistic match.

05

ITERATION

Compare outputs against references, identify drift or weaknesses, then adjust the prompt, source material or tool rather than accepting the first usable generation.

06

CONSISTENCY

Maintain continuity across characters, costumes, environments, lighting and visual language so assets belong to the same production.

07

MOTION

Test generated assets for animation and video use, considering stability, source-frame quality, movement and final format.

08

EDIT

Sequence and pace the generated assets within an NLE, treating them as traditional footage or frames to build the narrative.

09

SOUND

Apply sound design, foley, and music to ground the AI-generated visuals, add weight, and enhance the overall emotional impact.

10

DELIVERY + REUSE

Finalize outputs for multiple formats and document successful workflows, prompts, and seeds for future scalability and asset variants.

How I Evaluate A Tool

QUALITY

Does it produce usable output?

CONTROL

Can I reliably direct the result?

CONSISTENCY

Does it hold characters/style over multiple outputs?

SPEED

Does it improve production rather than add friction?

REUSE

Can the workflow scale to variants or future assets?

FIT

Is this actually the right tool for this particular job?
