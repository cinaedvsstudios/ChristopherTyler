BACKROOM

DIGITAL PLATFORM / PROMOTIONAL CONTENT / AUDIENCE EXPERIENCE

Backroom is a nightlife directory designed to make it easier to discover nightclubs, parties and nightlife venues across Europe.

The platform divides venues and events using searchable tags and categories, allowing users to quickly find nightlife that suits their interests. It also includes custom sharing lists, so users can collect and share selected venues or events with friends.

Alongside the platform itself, I create the visual advertising used to promote Backroom on Instagram, including campaign imagery and short-form video content designed for social formats.

The project combines product thinking, visual communication and audience-focused content, connecting the way information is structured on the platform with the way the brand is communicated publicly.

Deliverables

Nightlife directory · tagged discovery system · custom sharing lists · Instagram imagery · promotional video · digital campaign content

Focus

Audience experience · information design · social content · visual identity · digital promotion
