CREMATION COUNTDOWN

GAME DEVELOPMENT / RAPID PRODUCTION / INTERACTIVE STORYTELLING

Cremation Countdown began as a competition entry for a four-day game jam, developed from concept to playable experience within a very short production window.

After the competition, I continued developing the project, completing and expanding many of the elements that could not be finished during the original four-day build. It has since grown into a fully playable game, with expanded environments, characters, animation, audio, interface elements and gameplay systems.

The project demonstrates both rapid creative production and longer-term iteration: establishing a viable visual and gameplay direction under tight time pressure, then returning to refine, expand and complete the experience into a more polished finished product.

Deliverables

Game environments · characters · animation · UI · audio · gameplay systems · cutscenes

Focus

Rapid prototyping · iteration · interactive storytelling · multimedia production · finished delivery
