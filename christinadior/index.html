CHRISTINA DIOR

MUSIC VIDEO / PROMOTIONAL ART / PERFORMANCE VISUALS

Christina Dior is a music and performance project for which I created a full music video alongside promotional artwork used for live shows.

The video work required translating the identity and mood of the song into a complete visual sequence, combining imagery, pacing, editing and music-led storytelling rather than treating each shot as an isolated asset.

The accompanying show artwork extended that visual identity into audience-facing promotional material, demonstrating how one creative direction can be adapted across motion and static formats.

Music video · show artwork · promotional imagery · performance visuals
